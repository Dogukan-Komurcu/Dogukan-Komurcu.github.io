Number War is a number and route-planning puzzle. Review the rating in your storefront and the in-game features before downloading.

Store age rating

As checked on 20 October 2026, App Store Connect shows a general rating of 13+. Ratings can be displayed differently by country, region and Apple’s rating system; use the local rating on the download page.

Being a number puzzle does not mean the game is designed specifically for young children or belongs to the Kids category. Gameplay can include monster chases and time-sensitive decisions.

Ads and purchases

The download is free. The game includes ads and optional in-app purchases. Current iOS products are not subscriptions; permanent features and consumable point packs are available. Local price and contents are shown before Apple payment confirmation.

If you manage a child’s device, review Apple Screen Time purchase restrictions and Ask to Buy when applicable. Ad-free access and photo features are separate products.

Nicknames and rankings

Online rankings can show a nickname, country/flag code and game results to other players. Do not put contact details or private information in a nickname. Ranking lists may also include bot opponents.

Real players can be reported or blocked on this device. Account and ranking data can be deleted in Settings. See privacy choices and community rules for details.
