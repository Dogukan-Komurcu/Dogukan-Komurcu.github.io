More ways to find a solution.

Classic — Plan a route and clear each board.

Operations — Combine addition, subtraction, multiplication and division in the right order.

Exact — Work toward precise values.

Daily puzzle — Come back for a new board each day.

Operations, Exact and the daily puzzle unlock after your first three classic completions.
